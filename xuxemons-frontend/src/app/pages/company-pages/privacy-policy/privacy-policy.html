Privacy Policy

At Xuxemons we value your privacy. This page explains what data we collect, why we collect it, and how we protect it.

Data we collect

We collect the information necessary to provide the game experience: email address, username, in-game progress and preferences. We never share personal data with third parties without your consent.

How we use your data

We use data to authenticate your account, sync progress across devices, send optional notifications and improve our services. We may also use aggregated data for internal analytics.

Security

We apply reasonable technical and organizational security measures to protect your data. If you notice unauthorized access, please contact support.
